Projects

    Pick or upload a project

    FL Studio projects: each channel with piano-roll notes becomes a part. Choose a MuseScore instrument for each one, then convert.

    PDFs and photos of sheet music: the parts are found (by your AI endpoint, or OCR), the notes are read by a recognition model, and MuseScore writes the score and one file per part.

    Guitar and bass tab: photos of a tab are read by your AI endpoint; a saved Songsterr tab page (Ctrl+S, “HTML only”) downloads its tracks. Both become Guitar Pro and then a score.

    MusicXML and MIDI go straight through MuseScore. Tick .gp5 to also get a Guitar Pro file.

    Settings

    Saved on the server, so every device sees the same choices.

    AI endpoint

    Reads sheet-music pages (title, instrument names, which pages make up each part, clef and key) and suggests instruments for FL Studio channels. The notes themselves are read by homr, a recognition model that runs inside this app, so they don't depend on the AI.

    For sheet music, pick a model that can read images (a "vision" model). The key is kept on this server in data/settings.json and is never sent back to the browser.

    Favorite instruments

    Favorites appear first in every instrument list. Tap ☆ to add or remove one and ▶ to hear it. The first play of an instrument takes a few seconds while MuseScore renders it.
